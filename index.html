hustl✦ prototype

Teens aged 13 to 17 on Sydney's Northern Beaches do paid jobs for adults inside a circle of parents who know each other. Tap through the phone. Every button works.

Teen flow
1 Welcome 2 Sign up + parent consent 3 Browse jobs 4 Job details + offer 5 My jobs (timeline) 6 Rate the poster 7 Money jars 8 Profile + trust ring 9 Your circles
Parent flow
10 Run a circle 11 Post a job to it 12 Choose a teen
