hustl✦ prototype

Parents who know each other form a circle. Only the circle can post jobs, and only its teens can see them. Tap through the phone. Every button works.

Parent flow (Kate)
1 Welcome 2 Start a circle 3 Invite parents you know 4 Join with a code 5 Circle home 6 Post a job to the circle 7 Choose a teen 8 Approve Zoe’s bookings
Teen flow (Zoe)
9 Join via Mum 10 Jobs in my circles 11 Job details + offer 12 My jobs (timeline) 13 Rate the poster 14 Money jars 15 Profile
